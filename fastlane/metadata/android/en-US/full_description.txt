Organize your system infomation and activities as a plural.

· Manage your system members, and attach additional fields to the member infomation.
· View your system members like a social media profile, showing their associated activities.
· Record internal conversations as Chats, supporting replies, stickers, and basic Markdown formatting.
· Start an internal poll to gather system members' options.
· Attachs fronting infomation in the application.
· Optionally manage multiple systems.
· Fully local, no need to connect to a network service.
